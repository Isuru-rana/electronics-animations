Diode Knee Voltage and How a Voltmeter Measures

5 V supply, 10 kΩ pot, 330 Ω and a 1N4007. Every point shows its voltage. Each meter reads V(red) minus V(COM): with no current both sides of the resistor sit at the same voltage, so it reads 0 V. Drag the probes, record points and plot the diode curve.
